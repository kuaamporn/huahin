Privacy Policy – GSC Report

Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. Who we are

GSC Report is a small internal tool operated by The Precious Moment, Hua Hin, Thailand. It is used only by the owner of the website thepreciousmoment.com to prepare a monthly performance report. It is not offered to the public.

2. What the app does

Once a month the app reads Google Search Console data for the owner's own website (clicks, impressions, click-through rate, average position, and top queries, pages and countries) and writes it into a new Google Sheet in the owner's own Google Drive.

3. Information the app accesses

  • Google Search Console data for the owner's website, read-only.
  • Google Sheets and Google Drive, only to create the monthly report spreadsheet and file it in a folder in the owner's own Drive.

The app does not collect personal information from website visitors, customers or any other third party.

4. How information is used and shared

Information is used only to produce the report described above. It stays inside the owner's Google account. We do not sell, rent, share or transfer it to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Storage and retention

The app keeps no database of its own. Reports are stored as Google Sheets in the owner's Google Drive and remain there until the owner deletes them.

6. Your choices

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the report spreadsheets from Google Drive removes the stored data.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
